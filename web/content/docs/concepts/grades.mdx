## The formula

Computed per suite and per server version. Grades are never inherited between versions.

1. Take the suite's checks that are not advisory.
2. A check is scored if its verdict is `pass`, `part` or `fail`: 1, 0.5 or 0 points.
3. Percentage is points divided by scored checks.
4. Letter: A at 90 or above, B at 75, C at 60, D at 45, otherwise F.

If nothing is scored, no grade is produced. A letter is never invented.

## The critical cap

If any critical check has verdict `fail` and the raw letter is better than D, the grade is D and marked capped. A raw D or F is not changed. A `part` on a critical check does not cap.

The cap exists so a server that leaks another tenant's rows, shows raw personal data, or runs injected SQL cannot show a good letter by passing the easy checks.

| Case | Scored | Points | Raw | Final |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| core: poisoning fails, the other three pass | 4 | 3 | 75%, B | D, capped |
| db: three checks fail, two pass | 5 | 2 | 40%, F | F, cap does not apply |

Both rows are toy-server test fixtures, not real servers.

## Provisional and excluded

- A critical check that is `inconclusive` marks the grade provisional, shown as `*`: a critical question is unanswered.
- Non-advisory checks that are `inconclusive` or `n/a` leave the denominator and are listed in the result.
- Advisory checks never change the letter.

## Read it with care

With 4 or 5 scored checks, one check moves the percentage by 20 to 25 points. A letter on this few checks is coarse. The band edges are conventional school-style bands chosen for legibility, not derived from a risk model, and they can change before methodology 1.0. Read the per-check lines, not just the letter.

What a grade does not cover is listed under [known blind spots](/docs/methodology#known-blind-spots). A server's grade changes only when the server does: there is no way to request, negotiate or adjust one.

See [Verdicts](/docs/concepts/verdicts) for how each check line is decided and [Methodology](/docs/methodology) for the full text.
